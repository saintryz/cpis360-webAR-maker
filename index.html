Welcome, space explorer! 🚀

Point your camera at a printed marker to see the Solar System or a black hole appear in 3D.

  1. Print a marker. Tap a button below to download. Print on plain paper at any size — bigger is easier.
  2. Place it flat on a desk in good light, with white space around the black border.
  3. Point your camera at the marker. The 3D model appears on top!
